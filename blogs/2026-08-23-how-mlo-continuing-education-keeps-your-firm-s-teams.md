Compliance is not something a mortgage firm handles once and then forgets. It shows up in every loan your team originates, which means a single originator's lapsed training can create problems that reach well past that one desk. When a license slips out of good standing, pipelines stall, regulators start paying attention, and the audit trail looks thinner than anyone wants.
