What happens when a solo attorney is spending more time managing discovery, organizing case files, drafting routine documents, and tracking deadlines than actually handling the legal work that requires their judgment? For many small firms, the problem isn't poor organization or a lack of clients; it's simply that too many responsibilities are sitting with the same person.
