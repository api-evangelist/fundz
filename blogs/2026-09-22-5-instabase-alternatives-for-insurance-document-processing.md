Insurance document processing can become complicated quickly when carriers are dealing with multiple formats, inconsistent layouts, scanned records, and unstructured submissions. The right platform therefore depends on more than extraction accuracy alone; deployment options, verification, integration, and the amount of configuration required all matter. This guide compares five Instabase alternatives specifically through the lens of insurance document processing.
