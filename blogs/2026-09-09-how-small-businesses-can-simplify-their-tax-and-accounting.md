Tax and accounting responsibilities rank among the most consistently stressful parts of running a small business, especially for owners who started their business out of passion for their product or service, not because they wanted to become amateur accountants. Between tracking expenses, managing invoices, understanding deductions, and staying compliant with changing regulations, it is easy for tax matters to become an ongoing source of anxiety rather than a manageable routine task.
